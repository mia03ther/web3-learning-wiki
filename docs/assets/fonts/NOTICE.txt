Modifications: static Regular weight 400, normal style; subset to all 7445
GB2312 characters (including 6763 Han characters), all supported characters
in docs/ and overrides/, ASCII, general/CJK punctuation and fullwidth forms.
7740 encoded Unicode characters retained; all current document Han
characters are covered. Unsupported emoji/scripts use system fallback fonts.
Unused glyphs, hinting and layout features removed; kerning and common
ligatures retained. Outlines were not redesigned. Converted to WOFF2 with
fontTools/Brotli and renamed to Notebook Serif as a derivative webfont.

No Claude proprietary font is included. Georgia is not redistributed; it
may be selected from the reader's locally installed system fonts.

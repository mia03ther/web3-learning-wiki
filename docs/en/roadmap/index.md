# Roadmap

Turning "long-term learning → hands-on building → advanced study and a career in
the global Web3 ecosystem" into executable, iterable plans.

## In this section

- [**Learning Roadmap**](./learning-path.md): staged fundamentals, technology, practice and output goals, plus key milestones;
- [**Skills & Resources**](./skills-resources.md): three skill trees (engineering / finance / bridge), free courses, sources and tools;
- [**Profile Building**](./profile.md): language and standardized tests, research, competitions, internships, recommendations and portfolio;
- [**Graduate Study**](./graduate-study.md): objective comparison of related graduate programs and application preparation.

## Three priorities

1. **Foundations and grades are long-term currency** — build them from every practice session and course, there is no "later";
2. **Build transferable skills early**: English, programming, information retrieval and public expression — the earlier the habit, the smoother later;
3. **Soft background accumulates steadily**: projects, hackathons, open source, internships, research and referrals — pace yourself, no need to rush.

## How to use

> The roadmap is a directional anchor, not a fixed track: technology and the
> industry change, so revisit it regularly and revise against official material
> and your own progress. Always check the latest official pages for concrete
> projects and application requirements.

> Start with the [Learning Roadmap](./learning-path.md).

# On-chain Data

Data quality, querying and analysis workflows: from raw logs to reusable insight.

## Topics

- **Data sources**: RPC nodes, indexers and analytics platforms such as Dune / Nansen;
- **Data quality**: deduplication, normalisation, timestamps and address formats — the "dirty data" problems of the chain;
- **Query practice**: Dune SQL, dashboard building and common patterns;
- **Applications**: on-chain metrics, market recaps and AI × data.

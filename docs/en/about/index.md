# About This Wiki

<p class="w3-lead">
<b>Web3 Learning Wiki</b> is my Web3 × AI notebook. I collect what I learn about blockchain,
smart contracts and agents, along with notes from events, so friends can read and discuss it together.
The English pages provide a starting point; most of the notebook is in Chinese.
</p>

## Why it exists

New topics come with a lot of terminology, and useful material is scattered across
events, documentation and code. Writing a note helps me work out what I understand
and gives me somewhere to return when I need it again.

## How I learn

- Make something small, then fill in the gaps as they appear.
- Write down what worked, what failed and where the information came from.
- Read across languages and ecosystems.
- Use testnets and simulated environments while learning.
- Share notes and code so other people can point out mistakes.

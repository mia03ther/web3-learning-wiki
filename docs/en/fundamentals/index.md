# Fundamentals

The basics section for Web3 and AI × Web3 — industry jargon translated into
plain language, intuition first.

- [**Core Concepts**](./core-concepts.md): 40+ concepts sorted by "blockchain basics → finance (DeFi) → AI agents → security & proofs → ecosystem", with analogies and self-test questions;
- [**Glossary**](./glossary.md): homophones and colloquial terms from event transcripts, mapped to standard spelling with verification status.

## How to use

1. Reading Field Notes or industry articles and hit an unfamiliar word? Look it up here;
2. After learning a concept, try **explaining it in your own words** (Feynman method);
3. Don't aim for memorisation on the first pass — build a mental map, then come back repeatedly.

> These are "entry-level" explanations; for rigorous definitions, follow the
> systematic courses in [Skills & Resources](../roadmap/skills-resources.md).
